# 2026 international series and cross-region head-to-head

_Last updated 15/10/2026. All sources accessed 15/10/2026. Series data: `/home/claude/worlds2026/data/results_2026.json` (82 series; matrices below computed from it with a script, intra-region series excluded from the matrices)._

Method notes
- Direct Liquipedia / Leaguepedia event pages were unreadable or stale (cached pre-event) when fetched (Liquipedia FST/EWC/MSI pages: fetch errors or empty; Leaguepedia MSI: participants only). Results were therefore assembled from Wikipedia, Sheep Esports, Dot Esports, esports.gg, GosuGamers, Inven Global and Liquipedia (Americas Cup page only), cross-checked against each other. Every series in the JSON carries its source URLs.
- "Core" events = First Stand 2026, Americas Cup 2026, MSI 2026, Esports World Cup 2026. DCGI (Demacia Cup Global Invitational, mid-table teams, still in progress) is kept separate as `supplementary` and is NOT in the combined core matrix.
- Worlds 2026 itself (started 15/10/2026) is excluded.
- EWC group stage is GSL-style with Bo1 opening/winners matches and Bo3 elimination/decider matches; playoffs Bo3, Grand Final Bo5. Bo1 and Bo5 series are both counted as one "series", so matrices are shown with and without EWC Bo1 games.
- Team codes: AL = Anyone's Legend (written AG.AL by some sources), TLAW = Team Liquid Alienware, TSW = Team Secret Whales (listed as "Team Secret" on Wikipedia EWC page), DCG = Relove Deep Cross Gaming, LOS = MIBR.LOS / LØS, BRO = HANJIN BRION, KT = KT Rolster.

## 4. Where the data supports or contradicts the usual "LCK/LPL > LEC > others" narrative
- Supported: LCK is clearly top (22-9) and LCK/LPL teams won all three tier-1 international titles (BLG at FST, HLE at MSI, DK at EWC). LCP and CBLOL are at the bottom.
- Contradicted / nuance: (1) LCK-LPL is a coin flip (5-5), LPL is not clearly below LCK. (2) A LEC team made the final at both FST (G2, lost 1-3 to BLG) and EWC (KC, lost 0-3 to DK); G2 swept GEN 3-0 and BNK 3-0 at FST (LEC 2-0 vs LCK there). (3) LEC trails LCS head-to-head 1-2 (MSI sweeps by LYON over G2 and TLAW over KC). (4) LCS went 5-4 at MSI, bettering LPL's 3-3 and LEC's 3-4 there; LYON finished 3rd. (5) The LPL's EWC was weak outside Bo1 (1-3 in Bo3+ cross-region; AL, JDG, BLG all eliminated by LCK/LEC teams). (6) T1 had to come through the MSI Play-In (9-0 in games), then lost both of its competitive bracket series: 2-3 to BLG and 1-3 to G2 (it swept FURIA); at EWC T1 finished 4th (beat HLE 2-0, lost to KC 1-2 and GEN 1-2).
